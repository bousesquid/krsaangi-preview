Find your colour

Sarees are named the way weavers name them. Arakku is not simply red, and vadamalli is not simply purple. Choose a shade, or search in your own words and we will find its family.

Swatches are indicative. Handwoven and hand-dyed colour varies from piece to piece and screen to screen.
